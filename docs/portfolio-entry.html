Píšeme všemi deseti (Touch typing, Czech keyboard)

I have always thought primary school should teach typing on a computer alongside handwriting. When my daughter reached that age, I decided to fill the gap myself: a full touch-typing course for a nine-year-old on the Czech QWERTZ keyboard. The decisions about its design and lesson content came out of a literature search on the didactics of teaching typing and the psychology of learning and motivation in children.

Another reason I did not simply point her at one of the well-known online courses is what Czech and its keyboard layout do differently.

Node with no dependencies, runs offline, multiple users, a page for parents with progress and statistics.
